A LETTER FOR YOU

TO MY LOVE, MY FOREVER ❤️

Three years to us together.

Three years of loving you, learning you, annoying you, missing you, laughing with you, arguing with you, making memories with you… and somehow, after all this time, my heart still gets ridiculously happy because of you.

But somewhere along the way, something changed, I stopped looking at you and seeing only my girlfriend. My heart started looking at you like my wife. ❤️

Sometimes I look at those words and wonder how they can possibly hold everything we have lived together. Three years of conversations that went on longer than they were supposed to. Three years of laughter, random pictures, dates, teasing each other, little arguments, making up, missing each other, worrying about each other, and creating memories that probably looked ordinary to everyone else but somehow became priceless to me. And somewhere in the middle of all of that, you stopped being just someone I loved. You became someone I started imagining my life with. I don't think I could point to one particular day and say, “That's when it happened.” Maybe that's the beautiful thing about us. It happened slowly—in all those little moments when I wasn't paying attention. In the way seeing your name on my phone could instantly change my mood. In the random photo you sent without me even asking, while I sat there smiling at my screen like an idiot. 😭❤️.

In the places we went together.

In our stupid conversations that probably wouldn't make sense to anyone except us. In those moments when we argued, annoyed each other, and still somehow found our way back to one another. And especially in those moments when you simply looked at me and somehow made everything around me disappear.

ou have this incredibly special place in my life that I don't think words will ever completely explain. You're the person I want to tell when something good happens. You're the person I want beside me when life isn't so good. You're the person I want to laugh with over absolutely nothing and the person whose hand I want to hold when things aren't easy. I don't only want the beautiful days with you. I want the ordinary ones too. I want lazy days. Busy days. Bad-hair days. Random late-night food plans. Long drives with absolutely no destination. Silly disagreements about what we're going to eat. Thousands more pictures. Inside jokes that get progressively worse. Travelling to places we've only talked about. Celebrating each other's little victories. Being there when one of us isn't okay. I want to know every version of you that life brings—and I want you to know every version of me. Because when I say I want a future with you, I'm not imagining some perfect fairytale where nothing ever goes wrong. ❤️

I'm imagining a real life. Our life. A life where things sometimes get messy, where we sometimes disagree, where plans change and difficult days come—but at the end of those days, somehow, it's still you and me choosing each other. And since we're talking about our future… Can we please discuss your habit of looking absolutely unfair in a saree? 😭❤️ Because, madam, I'm trying very hard to behave like a normal boyfriend, and then you show up looking like someone's ridiculously beautiful wife and look at me that way. What exactly am I supposed to do with that? 👀 You call it wearing a saree.

I call it giving me unnecessary husband thoughts. 😂❤️

And maybe I joke about it now… but there is a very real part of my heart that hopes one day I won't have to joke about it anymore. I hope one day I'll look across a room, see you standing there just as beautiful, and realize that somehow the girl I once called my girlfriend is now the woman I get to call my wife. And I think that is what I want you to understand most from this letter. I don't love you only for the memories we've already made. I love you for all the life I still hope we get to experience together. I want to watch us grow. I want to see who you become five, ten, twenty years from now—and I want to be standing there, probably still annoying you, still flirting with you, still stealing glances at you when you aren't looking, and still wondering how I got so lucky. ❤️ Years from now, I hope we open these photographs again. Maybe we'll laugh at our hairstyles. Maybe we'll argue about who looked better. Maybe you'll catch me staring at that saree picture again and say, “Seriously? Still?” And I'll probably say: “Of course. Have you seen my wife?” 😌❤️

I want them to be the first few pages of a very, very long one. There are still so many things I want with you. More sunsets. More photographs. More birthdays. More anniversaries. More ridiculous conversations. More adventures. More quiet evenings. More celebrations. More hugs after difficult days. More “I miss you.” More “I love you.” More of everything that makes us us. And one day, hopefully, fewer goodbyes and a lot more “I'm home.” ❤️ I can't promise that I'll always have the perfect words. I can't promise that I'll never annoy you—actually, we both know there's absolutely no chance of that. 😂 I can't promise that every day will be easy. But I can tell you what my heart wants: To keep choosing you. Not just today. Not just because it's our anniversary. Not just while everything feels romantic and beautiful. But through every season of life that we're lucky enough to share. Because after three years, after everything we've experienced, after all the different versions of us that have existed… I still look at you and want more time. More memories. More years. More us. And someday, if life is kind to us, I hope I get to look at you and realize that the girl who once occupied such a special little place in my heart somehow became the person beside whom I built my entire life. So, my love… Happy 3rd Anniversary. ❤️

Thank you for the memories behind us, for the love we have today, and for giving my heart someone worth dreaming about tomorrow with. And if you ever wonder how long I want this story to last…

if you ever ask me how long I want to love you… Forever still wouldn't feel long enough. ❤️ I love you forever. 😘😘😘

Happy 3rd Anniversary, my almost-wife. “I fell for my girlfriend, but somewhere along the way, my heart started dreaming about my wife.” 😘❤️

“In every lifetime, in every version of this world, I’d still find my way to you—and I’d still choose you.” ❤️.

Yours—completely, ridiculously, and hopefully forever,
Shubham ❤️
Your Husband
